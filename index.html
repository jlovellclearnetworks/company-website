The network behind the building.

Clear Networks designs, installs and supports the wired and wireless infrastructure inside commercial properties — along with the firewalls, switching and day-to-day support that keep it running.

What we do

  • Network infrastructure

    Switching, routing and VLAN design for base building and tenant networks — new installs, refreshes, and untangling what was left behind by whoever came before.

  • Firewalls and perimeter security

    Edge firewall deployment and configuration, secure remote access, and segmentation between building systems, tenant networks and corporate infrastructure.

  • Wireless

    Coverage design and controller-managed access points for offices, common areas, amenity floors and back-of-house.

  • Day-to-day IT support

    Workstations, email, printing and the practical problems a site runs into. Break-fix when something breaks, project work when something needs replacing.

  • Vendor coordination

    Working alongside carriers, building systems integrators and tenant IT so that the network side of a project isn't the part that stalls it.

Who we work with

Our work is concentrated in commercial property — office buildings and the management teams that run them. The problems tend to arrive sideways: a circuit that was never documented, a tenant fit-out on a deadline, a system that stopped talking to the one beside it.

Most of our engagements are long-running, and some have been in place for more than a decade. We are small, we answer our own phone, and the person who scoped the work is the person who shows up to do it.
